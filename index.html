Why DNA Coffee

Coffee knowledge, taught the way it should be learned.

We treat coffee skill the way it's actually built — in small, deliberate parts. Every class isolates one part of the craft, from the chemistry of extraction to the muscle memory of pouring latte art, so you leave with something you can repeat, not just something you watched.

Classes run in small groups with real equipment, real green beans, and real cupping tables — taught by people who work with coffee every day, not just teach it.
